1 Overview

Easter Bunny Runner (the "Game", package utiledge.easter.bunny.easterbasket.easteregg.bunnycallsimulator) is a single-player arcade game. We designed it to be privacy-friendly: your game progress stays on your own device, and we do not run accounts, logins, or leaderboards that upload your data.

This policy explains the limited data the Game uses, the Android permissions it requests, and the third-party services it relies on: Google AdMob (ads) and Google Firebase (analytics, crash reports and push notifications).

2 Information we collect

Game progress (stored only on your device). The Game saves your settings and progress locally using Android's on-device storage (DataStore). This information never leaves your phone and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. It includes:

  • Your high score and total number of runs
  • In-game currency you collect (coins and diamonds)
  • Unlocked & selected bunny skins and earned achievements
  • Your preferences: sound, music, haptics, chosen difficulty, and whether you've seen the tutorial
All of the above is stored privately within the app's own sandbox on your device. Uninstalling the Game removes it.

Advertising ID (Google AdMob). The Game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects your device's Android Advertising ID together with basic device and app information (such as device model, OS version, IP address and ad interactions). Ads may be personalised depending on your settings. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on newer Android versions).

Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics). The Game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how it is used, for example which screens are opened and how runs go (score, coins collected). This data is collected with the Firebase app-instance ID and, where available, the Advertising ID. It is not linked to your name or any account.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the Game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report (stack trace, device model, OS version and app version) so we can fix the problem.

Push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging). If notifications are enabled, Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns your installation a device token so notifications can be delivered. This token is generated and handled by Google's Firebase service. The Game does not collect a profile, contact list, or personal identity from this.

3 What we do not collect

We want to be clear about what the Game does not do:

  • No personal information — no name, email, phone number, or address
  • No hardware identifiers such as IMEI or serial number
  • No location data
  • No contacts, call logs, photos, or files
  • No account or login — you play completely anonymously
  • No leaderboard or score upload — scores stay on your device
  • No selling of personal data — the only data used for ads is what Google AdMob collects, described above
Your game progress is never uploaded; it stays on your device.

4 Permissions & why we ask

Android requires apps to declare permissions. Here is every permission the Game uses and exactly why:

InternetNeeded to load ads (Google AdMob), send analytics and crash reports (Firebase), and receive push notifications. The Game has no backend of its own.
NotificationsLets the Game show you notifications (for example, reminders or news). You can turn these off in your system settings at any time.
CameraUsed only for the optional, playful in-app "bunny video call" self-view. The camera preview is shown live on your screen and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. If you deny the permission, the feature simply shows a placeholder avatar instead.
VibrateProvides haptic feedback (little buzzes) on hits, pickups, and menu taps. Optional — toggle it off in settings.
Advertising IDLets Google AdMob read the Android Advertising ID to serve and measure ads (see "Information we collect").
The camera is released as soon as you leave the call screen. No video frames are saved to disk or uploaded anywhere.

5 Third-party services

The Game uses the following Google services, which process the data described above. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

  • Google AdMob — shows ads; collects the Advertising ID and device information.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — usage statistics.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications.

6 Children's privacy

Easter Bunny Runner is a family-friendly arcade game suitable for all ages. It does not create accounts or require any sign-in, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the advertising and analytics data described above. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help.

7 Data retention & deletion

Because your game data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • Clearing the app's data from your system settings resets your progress.
  • Uninstalling the Game permanently removes all locally stored data.
  • Turning off notifications in your system settings stops the push token from being used.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept by Google Firebase under its standard retention settings. To ask for it to be deleted, contact us below.
